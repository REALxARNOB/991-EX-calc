## 2024-05-15 - ARIA Labels for Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons or buttons using abstract symbols (like the "C" for clear or "<-" for backspace) lack text context, making them opaque to screen reader users and confusing for standard users. Accessibility standards mandate `aria-label` attributes to provide necessary context.
**Action:** Always add descriptive `aria-label`s to any buttons relying primarily on icons or non-alphabetic text representations.
